The shirts

Fourteen sites, each photographed on the shirt it ships on, and the same fourteen again as the dark line: white ink on a dark garment, shown as mockups built from those photographs. Every site has its own garment colour, so the deck is the palette of the line.

Colours are the garment colours each site was printed on; the final run may add or swap a colour per site. Get the launch email to hear when yours ships, or make your own: any design, any colour, your line or logo.

The designs

Every plate, drawn by hand from the dive itself. Tap one for the full back print, facts and all. Previews are watermarked and low resolution; the shirts carry the full art.
