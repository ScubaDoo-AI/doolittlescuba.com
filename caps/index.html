The hats

Flexfit 6277 fitted caps, the dark line. Every cap is embroidered on three sides — the site front (dive flag, name and coordinates) across the front panels, the DS mark small on the left side, “Do - A - Little Scuba” arched across the back. Nothing is printed. Home Port is made to order with your own port and coordinates; the four brand caps carry the marks instead of a site. S/M and L/XL. And one beanie: The Mark on the cuff of a Stanley/Stella rib-knit beanie in French Navy, one size. The shark mark stays on the Signature Series shirts. Run across the tabs to pull a cap forward; open it for the front, the back and the details.

These are mockups, not photographs: the first caps are being sampled now. Site caps are $38, brand caps $36, the Home Port cap $48 — all embroidered. Any cap with any tee is $8 off the two apart. Any of them can be changed on the Make it yours page: a different mark, your own line on the back.
