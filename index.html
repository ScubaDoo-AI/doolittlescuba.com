The hats

Flexfit 6277 fitted caps, the dark line. Every cap comes embroidered — the DS mark small on the left side, “Do - A - Little Scuba” across the back — and the site caps get their chest block printed in white on the front when you order. Home Port is made with your own port and coordinates; the four brand caps are embroidered front, side and back. S/M and L/XL. The shark mark stays on the Signature Series shirts. Run across the tabs to pull a cap forward; open it for the front, the back and the details.

These are mockups, not photographs: the first caps are being sampled now. Site caps are $35 (embroidered cap, site block printed to order), brand caps $40 (embroidered throughout). Any of them can be changed on the Make it yours page: a different mark, your own line on the back.

The stickers

Die-cut vinyl for the tank, the dive box and the logbook. Achievement Unlocked writes each course as a game achievement — twelve of them, $4 each or $15 for the full set on one sheet. The classic die-cuts are $5, one for each course and specialty, about 3 inches — plus the brand set: the round DS Seal, the stacked and horizontal logos, and the Do - A - Little Scuba tagline stacked, in dive gear and as a bumper sticker. Waterproof, with the DS mark worked into every one. More on the way.

Every mark by hand

Each design starts with a dive. The site is drawn line by line by a PADI Master Diver who has been there, then checked against published charts, reef records and the dive log before it goes to press.

No stock art and no generated images. The signature shark on the sleeve, the images on the chest and every label on the back all come from the same hand.
