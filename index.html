Every mark by hand

Each design starts with a dive. The site is drawn line by line by a PADI Master Scuba Diver who has been there, then checked against published charts, reef records and the dive log before it goes to press.

No stock art and no generated images. The signature shark on the sleeve, the images on the chest and every label on the back all come from the same artist.
