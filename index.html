The hats

Pigment-dyed dad caps to go with the shirts. Every site gets its chest block on the front, the shark mark on the side and the tagline across the back; five more carry the marks on their own. Tap a cap for the full mockup, or flip it to see the back.

These are mockups, not photographs: the first caps are being sampled now. Any of them can be changed on the Make it yours page: another colour, a different mark, your own line on the back.

Every mark by hand

Each design starts with a dive. The site is drawn line by line by a PADI Master Diver who has been there, then checked against published charts, reef records and the dive log before it goes to press.

No stock art and no generated images. The signature shark on the sleeve, the images on the chest and every label on the back all come from the same hand.
