The workshop

Queen Anne's Revenge

Apr–Dec 2025 · 16 photos

A worn-out EZGO, bought in April. By June: lithium power, a lift, the hull painted with gun ports, a hand-drawn treasure map on the hood, a ship's wheel, candle lanterns on the roof posts and a Blackbeard flag.

Reef tanks and the stone stand

2013 → today · 17 photos

Reef-keeping since 2013. The current nano reef sits on a stand carved from foam board, stone by stone, sealed and painted to read as a dry-stacked wall, and it is fed from a two-barrel saltwater mix reservoir plumbed under the carport.

Skull and horn mounts

2015 → 2026 · 16 photos

Twenty-five European-style mounts on hand-cut, stained plaques with leather pads. Every pad is laser-engraved with Eric's signature and every mount is named with a copper tag he stamped by hand, letter by letter.

The tags, as stamped:

On the same wall: a hat rack made from a longhorn skull with two hand-woven 7 ft bullwhips and a wolf paw; a two-necklace voodoo set of boar tusks, mink femur bones, coyote toe, buffalo teeth bleached and unbleached, coyote teeth, wooden beads and custom skull beads; and a rose crystal knife with a reindeer antler handle. The knife is another maker's; the mounting and embellishments are Eric's.

The truck

2019 → 2026 · 13 photos

A Silverado, worked on in stages: sub box, under-glow, lift and light bar, grille guard, bed lights, roof rack, a fibre-optic star headliner and a gauge cluster swap.

Electronics, drones and FPV

2010 → today · 9 photos

From a 2010 breadboard and a seven-segment timer to a drone fleet and a self-built FPV camera rig: a DJI O3 air unit, pan-tilt gimbal and cooling fan on an acrylic plate, finished October 2026.
