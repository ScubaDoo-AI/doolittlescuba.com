Every Doolittle Scuba tee, cap, beanie and jacket is printed or embroidered to order. If something is wrong because of us, we'll make it right at no cost to you. Because each item is made for your order, we can't take it back for a change of mind.

If something is wrong with your order

We will send a free replacement, or refund you in full, if your item arrives:

  • damaged, defective or misprinted,
  • different from what you ordered (wrong design, size or color, or a name or line of text that doesn't match what you typed), or
  • not at all (see Lost packages in our Shipping Policy).

Email shop@doolittlescuba.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. You do not need to send the item back unless we ask. Replacements are subject to availability; if a design or size is sold out, we'll refund you.

Change of mind and size exchanges

Item type Returns or exchanges for change of mind or wrong size ordered
Tees, caps, beanies and jackets (printed or embroidered to order) Not accepted. Each one is made only after you order it, so it can't be restocked.
Personalized and custom (names, "Make it yours" designs, your own line of text) Not accepted. These are made only for you and can't be resold.
Sticker packs and Achievement stickers Not accepted once mailed.
Sale or clearance items Not accepted.

Please check the size chart on each product page before ordering. If you're between sizes, email us before you order and we'll help you choose.

Personalized and custom items

  • We print exactly what you type. Please check spelling and capitals before you order. A typo in what you entered is not a misprint; email us within 12 hours of ordering and we'll fix it before it goes to print.
  • Long names are fitted to the design. A name longer than the design is drawn for is printed smaller or set on two lines, as explained on the product page before you agree to it. A resized name that matches what you agreed to is not a defect. If we can't make a name read well, we'll email you before printing it.
  • We review every custom design before it prints. We may decline text that is vulgar, offensive or uses someone else's trademark, and will refund that item in full.
  • Embroidery digitization fee. The one-time fee to turn your text into stitches is refunded if you cancel before production starts. Once your design has been digitized it is not refundable, but it's never charged again for the same design.
  • If we misprint a personalized item, we remake it with the same names or text at no cost to you.

Cancellations

  • All orders, including personalized and custom: email us within 12 hours of ordering and we'll cancel for a full refund. Once an order is in production or has shipped, it can't be cancelled.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Your bank may take another 5–10 days to show it. Refunds include the sales tax you paid. Original shipping is refunded only when the problem was our error. If we ask you to send an item back, we refund once it reaches us.

In-person purchases

Items bought from us in person at events or dive sites follow this same policy. Please inspect your item before you leave; at events we're happy to swap a size on the spot while stock lasts.

Wholesale and commissioned work

This policy covers retail purchases. Wholesale orders (including dive shop and logo orders) and commissioned designs follow the terms in their own written quote or agreement.
