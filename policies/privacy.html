Doolittle Scuba L.L.C. ("Doolittle Scuba," "we," "us") runs doolittlescuba.com and the Doolittle Scuba Designs online store (together, the "Services"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We are a small, family-run business: we collect only what we need to make and ship your order, and we do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Category Examples When
Contact and shipping details Name, email, shipping and billing address, phone if you give it When you order, join our email list or email us
Order information What you bought, returned or cancelled, and when When you order
Payment information Card type and last four digits only When you pay; full card numbers are handled by our payment processor and never seen or stored by us
Communications Messages, photos and details you send us When you contact us, e.g. for a return
Device and usage information Browser, device type, pages viewed, referring link, approximate location from IP address Automatically when you visit, through Shopify and the analytics described below

How we use it

  • To make, ship and support your order, including returns, replacements and delivery problems.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To send order and shipping emails.
  • To send our newsletter and drop announcements, only if you sign up. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which pages and links people use, so we can improve the site.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to enforce our terms.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only what each needs:

  • Shopify hosts our store, processes payments (including in-person card payments at events), calculates tax and sends order and marketing emails. Shopify also processes information under its own Consumer Privacy Policy, and you can exercise certain rights through the Shopify Privacy Portal.
  • Printful, our print partner, receives your name, shipping address and order details to make and ship made-to-order items.
  • USPS and other carriers receive your name and shipping address to deliver your order.
  • Umami, our website analytics provider on doolittlescuba.com, counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you personally.
  • Our email and accounting tools, to answer your messages and keep business records.
  • Authorities or others when required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

Shopify may also enable advertising and personalization features that use information about your activity. You can opt out of that sharing on our Data Sharing Opt-Out page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out for that browser. We do not respond to other "Do Not Track" signals.

Cookies

Our Shopify store uses cookies needed to run the cart and checkout, and may use cookies for analytics. You can control cookies in your browser settings; blocking some may stop checkout from working. Our analytics on doolittlescuba.com do not use cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for at least seven years for tax and accounting purposes. We keep email-list information until you unsubscribe, and other information only as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Your choices and rights

  • Marketing email: unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by emailing us.
  • Access, correction and deletion: email us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We may need to verify your identity, and we may keep records the law requires us to keep.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as portability or appealing our decision on a request. Email us and we'll respond within the time your state's law requires. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

We use reputable platforms with encrypted checkout and limit access to your information to the two people who run the business. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't email us card numbers or other sensitive details.

Children

Our Services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Links to other sites

Our site links to dive sites, dive shops and social media. Their privacy practices are their own; please review their policies.

Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell customers by email.
