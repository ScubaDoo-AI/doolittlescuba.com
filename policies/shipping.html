Doolittle Scuba L.L.C. ("Doolittle Scuba," "we," "us") ships to addresses in the United States, including APO/FPO/DPO addresses. We do not ship internationally at this time.

How long until it ships

Everything we sell is made to order, so processing time comes before transit time. Nothing is printed until you order it.

Item type Made by Ships
Tees, caps, beanies and jackets Our print partner, printed or embroidered to order Usually 2–7 business days after you order
Personalized and custom (names, "Make it yours", your own line of text) Our print partner, after we check your design Usually 3–8 business days. Embroidered caps can take a few days longer.
Sticker packs and Achievement stickers Us, from stock we keep on hand Within 3 business days

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

Carrier, cost and tracking

Tees, caps, beanies and jackets ship from our print partner by ground service (usually USPS or UPS), which typically arrives 2–5 business days after it ships. Shipping cost is shown at checkout. You will receive an email with tracking as soon as each package ships.

Sticker mail. Sticker packs and achievement stickers that we ship ourselves go by USPS letter mail in a plain envelope, without tracking, and usually arrive 3–7 business days after mailing. Sticker mail costs $1.00, or is free when those stickers total $10 or more. We email you when your stickers are mailed. If your order also includes other items, the sticker rate is added to their shipping.

Caps and beanies are embroidered at a different facility from shirts, so a tee and a cap ordered together arrive as two packages, each with its own tracking email. Stickers come from us in their own envelope. You will not be charged extra shipping for a split we choose to make.

Production delays

If your order can't ship within the window above, we will email you with a new estimate. You may cancel for a full refund at any time before production starts; after that, you may still cancel and be refunded if we can't give you a firm date.

Address problems

Please check your shipping address at checkout. Email us within 12 hours of ordering if you need to change it; once an order is in production we may not be able to. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect or incomplete address, we will reship it once you pay the new postage, or refund the item price (not shipping) if you prefer.

Lost, late or stolen packages

  • No movement: If tracking hasn't updated in 7 days, or your order hasn't arrived within 10 business days of its estimated delivery date, email us. We'll trace it and, if it's lost, send a replacement or a refund at no charge.
  • Sticker mail (no tracking): If your stickers haven't arrived within 15 business days of the mailing email, email us and we'll send a replacement at no charge.
  • Claims window: Please report a lost package within 30 days of its estimated delivery date.
  • Marked delivered but not received: Check with neighbors and your local post office first, then email us. We will help you file a claim with USPS. We can't replace packages the carrier confirms were delivered to the address entered at checkout.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated and collected at checkout as required by the state you ship to.
