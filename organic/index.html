The organic line

One organic edition of every design, in the organic colour closest to its regular shirt: black ink on the light ones, white on the dark. The photographs here are the regular shirts re-dyed to the organic colour; samples are on their way.

Why organic

Organic cotton is grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilisers, from non-GMO seed. The tees are Stanley/Stella Creator 2.0: 100% organic ring-spun combed cotton, 180 g/m², made in a fair-wear audited factory. The caps are Econscious EC7000 organic cotton; the beanie is Atlantis 100% organic rib. Every piece is printed or embroidered to order, so nothing is made that nobody wears.

The organic edition costs the same as the regular one. We would rather you chose the better shirt than paid for it.
