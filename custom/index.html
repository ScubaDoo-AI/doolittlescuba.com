Custom shirts

Make it yours

Pick a colour, pick a design, put it where you want it. Add a line of your own or your logo, see it on the shirt, and send us the request.

Every shirt is still printed by hand in Newnan, one at a time, on the same Comfort Colors 1717 tee as the catalog. A personalized tee is $47, or $48 with your own line ($3 more in 2XL and 3XL); a personalized cap (Flexfit 6277, S/M or L/XL) is $40, or $41 with your line, plus a one-time $7 to digitize your line for embroidery ($9.95 for a line on the front and the back), charged once per design however many caps. Logo shirts are for dive shops, clubs and resellers: your logo on the chest, the DS mark on the sleeve, priced by quantity. We reply with a proof before anything is printed.

← Back to the shirts

Back designs go on the back: the dive-site plates, the Certified-ish backs and the Off-World Hunt Club lines (full back only). A Certified-ish design can go on the front of a shirt as well. Either way it runs full size: full front or full back, never the pocket or the upper back.

Chest blocks (site name, flag and coordinates) go on the chest.

The seal goes on the chest or a sleeve. DS marks go anywhere.

Your line or logo can take the chest, a sleeve or the upper back.

The shark mark is for Signature Series shirts only: a tee with a dive-site plate on the back can carry the shark mark, the DS mark, or both. Everything else carries the DS mark, wherever you put it. If you take the last mark off, it comes back on the sleeve (or the side of a cap). On a shirt the sleeve mark sits at the bottom of the sleeve, along the arm hole.
