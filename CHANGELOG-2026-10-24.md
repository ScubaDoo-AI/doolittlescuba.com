# DoolittleScuba.com — update of 2026-10-24

## Changed
- **The hats are the dark line.** The hats section now shows the 19 Flexfit 6277 caps that are in the store (14 site caps and Home Port, DTF in white ink, $35; The Mark, The Seal, The Stack and The Wordmark embroidered, $40; S/M and L/XL), with the front and back renders from the store copied to `img/hats/dark/` (WebP plus 600 px thumbnails). Chips: Site caps (15) | Brand caps (4). The 19 light pigment-dyed dad caps are off the page (archived in the store; their colours do not exist in the 6277). The old renders stay under `img/hats/` for now, nothing links them.
- **Deck on touch screens:** the neighbouring cards no longer shift sideways when a card steps forward (that read as a spring while swiping); they still part on hover devices.
- **The shirts and the hats as decks of cards** (home page, `#shirts` and `#hats`). Each line is a row of garment-coloured tabs, the name running up the tab, one card per shirt or cap; the last card shows in full. Run across the tabs (hover) or swipe (phone: the card nearest the middle of the screen) and that card steps forward, lifts, and its tag appears above it: line, number, name, place, colour, status, price, Open. Click the card or its tag and the item opens in place of the deck: the photo or mockup with Front / Back / The art, the spec sheet, place, coordinates and the plate fact for sites, and the launch-news / Make it yours / site-guide buttons, with previous / next and "Back to the deck". Chips switch the line: Signature Series (14) | Certified-ish (20) on the shirts, Site caps (14) | Brand caps (5) on the hats. Keyboard: Tab moves along the deck, Enter opens. The old grids stay in the page as the no-JS fallback and the data source; the Certified-ish tees joined `#sh-grid` (mockups from `img/certs/`, with new 600 px thumbnails). The Collections buttons now open the deck on their line, with "The plates →" / "The art →" links to the designs grid. Umami: `deck-line`, `shirt-open` / `hat-open` with `via: deck`, `deck-launch-news`, `deck-custom`, `deck-guide`.
- **Signature Series back prints.** Each dive-site plate now has a watermarked preview of the whole back print — the plate with its Facts / Species / Water block — made from the print files, with the same treatment as the Certified-ish finals: `img/designs/<slug>-back.webp` (900×1240, diagonal credit watermark and footer) and `custom/art/<slug>-back.webp` (alpha line art, watermark baked in). The plate-only previews (`img/designs/<slug>.webp`) stay as they were; the plate drawing in the back print is identical to them.
- **Home, the designs grid:** every card with artwork opens in the shirt viewer. A plate opens its full back print; a Certified-ish card opens its full design. Thumbnails unchanged; intro line says so. Umami event `design-open` with the design slug.
- **Site guides, "On the shirt":** the back print sits beside the two shirt photos, captioned; stacks under them on a phone.
- **Make it yours:** a plate at "full back" now shows the full back print (plate, facts, species, water) scaled to the torso, as the shirts are printed; "upper back, smaller" keeps the plate alone. Certified-ish backs unchanged.

- **Make it yours — prices.** A personalized tee is priced on the page as you build it: $43 each (the $38 tee + $5) for your design and colour, $44 (+ $6) once a line of your own or your logo goes on the shirt; $3 more in 2XL and 3XL, as the catalog. The price box above the sizes shows the per-item price, why, and the running total; the request email carries the same line. Caps follow the same rule on the $32 cap: $37, or $38 with your line or logo. The intro says the prices up front.
- Fix: the Caps quantity box no longer shows in shirt mode (`.field{display:flex}` was overriding `hidden`).
